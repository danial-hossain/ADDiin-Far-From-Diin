1. PROJECT TITLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ad-Diin: An AI-Integrated Islamic Digital Platform with Transparent Welfare 
Donations, Digital Habit Focus, and RAG-Powered Knowledge Assistance


2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & UN SDG ALIGNMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Problem Statement
In today's digital era, Muslims often need to use multiple separate applications 
and websites for different aspects of their daily religious life. Prayer times, 
Hijri calendars, Zakat calculation, charitable donations, Islamic programs, 
personal religious habits, and Islamic knowledge are generally available through 
disconnected platforms. This fragmentation makes it difficult for users to 
access these essential services from a single, reliable source.

Another major challenge is the lack of transparency in digital charitable giving. 
Donors do not always have a clear, verifiable mechanism to track their digital 
transactions or observe how much funds have been collected for a particular 
charitable cause. This lack of visible accountability reduces donor trust and 
discourages digital community welfare participation.

Digital distraction is another growing challenge. Excessive use of social media 
and addictive websites consumes valuable time and significantly reduces users' 
focus on religious, educational, and productive activities. Existing productivity 
tools focus exclusively on generic workplace time management rather than helping 
users maintain consistent, spiritually enriching Islamic routines.

Furthermore, Muslims seeking authentic Islamic knowledge online frequently 
encounter unverified information, fabricated Hadith, or AI-generated responses 
lacking reliable citations. General-purpose Large Language Models (LLMs) often 
hallucinate plausible-sounding but religiously inaccurate information. Providing 
strictly source-grounded Islamic guidance with verifiable Quranic and Sahih 
Hadith references is therefore a critical necessity.

To address these problems, Ad-Diin proposes a unified, AI-integrated Islamic 
digital platform that combines daily Islamic utilities, transparent charitable 
giving, digital focus/well-being management, personalized "My Deen" habit 
tracking, and a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) AI assistant grounded in 
verified Quranic verses and authentic Hadith sources.

3. PROJECT DESCRIPTION & SOLUTION APPROACH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Project Description
Ad-Diin is an all-in-one AI-integrated Islamic web ecosystem developed using 
ASP.NET Core (.NET 9) MVC. It centralizes essential religious utilities, 
humanitarian giving, digital well-being, and verified Islamic knowledge into a 
high-performance, secure web platform.

The platform provides real-time prayer schedules with next-Waqt countdown clocks, 
Sehri and Iftar timings, and an interactive Hijri calendar. It includes an automated 
Zakat Calculator with dynamic Gold and Silver Nisab reference benchmarks and a 
transparent donation portal integrated with SSLCommerz. For digital health, 
Ad-Diin introduces custom focus sessions that help users block distracting websites 
and dedicate time to educational or spiritual pursuits. The "My Deen" module 
gamifies spiritual consistency with a digital Dhikr counter, daily habit tracking, 
points, goals, and achievement badges. 

At the technological core of the platform is Diin AI, a conversational Islamic 
assistant that uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to generate responses 
strictly based on verified Quranic verses and authentic Hadith collections.

3.2 Solution Approach & System Architecture
The system follows a multi-tier, decoupled software architecture:

1. Presentation Layer (ASP.NET Core MVC Razor Views):
   - Fully responsive, user-friendly UI built with Bootstrap 5.3, custom styling, 
     and asynchronous AJAX interactions.
2. Application & Business Logic Layer (C# .NET 9 Service Interfaces):
   - Implements Dependency Injection (DI) with dedicated services: IPrayerTimeService, 
     IDonationService, IMyDeenService, IFocusService, and IDiinAIService.
3. Data Access Layer (Entity Framework Core & Microsoft SQL Server):
   - Code-First database design with migrations, parameterized LINQ queries, 
     and ASP.NET Core Identity for PBKDF2 password hashing and role authorization.
4. Payment Gateway Subsystem (SSLCommerz):
   - Secure payment initiation, IPN validation, automated transaction logging, 
     and verifiable TranId receipt generation.
5. External RAG AI Backend (Google Colab / Dedicated GPU Server):
   - Decoupled FastAPI server running Qwen3-8B (4-bit quantized via bitsandbytes) 
     and ChromaDB vector database.
   - Embeddings: sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2.
   - Strict Context-Only RAG prompt enforcing verbatim citations (Surah name, 
     Ayah number, Hadith reference) and fallback messages for out-of-context queries.


4. KEY FUNCTIONALITIES / FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Daily Islamic Utilities
- Real-time prayer timings (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Jummah).
- Dynamic live countdown clock to the upcoming prayer (Waqt).
- Automated Sehri and Iftar schedules with Ramadan alerts.
- Live Gregorian-to-Hijri calendar conversion and Islamic milestone reminders.

4.2 Dynamic Zakat Calculator
- Automated calculation of Zakat liabilities across diverse wealth categories 
  (Cash, Gold, Silver, Investments, Business Goods, minus short-term debts).
- Real-time reference thresholds for Gold Nisab (87.48g) and Silver Nisab (612.36g).
- Transparent 2.5% calculation output with breakdown explanations.

4.3 Transparent Digital Donation Portal
- Multi-category philanthropic support (Zakat, Orphan Care, Winter Clothing, 
  Iftar Drives, Disaster Relief, and General Sadaqah).
- Seamless online payments via SSLCommerz (Mobile Banking, Cards, Net Banking).
- Unique verifiable Transaction Token (TranId) with instant digital receipts.
- Live public fund metrics and category-wise visual breakdowns to foster donor trust.

4.4 Islamic Programs & Activities Management
- Discovery portal for Islamic webinars, Quran/Hadith study circles, and youth events.
- Online booking and registration for educational activities, Milad, and Dua requests.
- Administrative review, capacity management, and participant status tracking.

4.5 Digital Focus & Time Management (Digital Well-being)
- Custom focus mode allowing users to block selected distracting/social media domains.
- Structured focus sessions with timers to dedicate undisturbed time to Quran study, 
  work, or reflection.
- Analytics and history on focused sessions to promote healthy digital routines.

4.6 My Deen — Personalized Islamic Habit Tracking
- Interactive digital Dhikr counter with milestone vibration/audio feedback.
- Daily Islamic habit checklist (5 daily prayers, morning/evening Adhkar, Quran recitation).
- Gamified spiritual habit tracking: Goal setting, consistency points, and achievement badges.

4.7 Diin AI — Verified RAG-Powered Islamic Assistant
- Bilingual natural language query resolution in Bengali and English.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) constraining responses strictly to verified texts.
- Guaranteed references citing exact Surah name, Ayah number, and Hadith numbers.
- Automated off-topic filtering to preserve religious focus and avoid hallucinations.

6.2 Research and Development Gaps Identified
1. High Ecosystem Fragmentation:
   Users are forced to install 4-5 different apps for daily prayers, Zakat calculation, 
   charity, spiritual habit logging, and productivity. Ad-Diin consolidates them into 
   a single cohesive web platform.

2. Neglect of Islamic Digital Well-being:
   Existing productivity tools (e.g., generic Pomodoro or site blockers) are not 
   oriented around Islamic mindfulness. Ad-Diin pioneers digital focus tools tied 
   to spiritual and educational balance.

3. Lack of Gamified Spiritual Motivation:
   Most Islamic apps provide static counters. Ad-Diin integrates positive reinforcement 
   (points, milestones, badges) to encourage consistent daily worship.

4. Unreliable Religious Answers in Modern AI:
   Standard generative AI models hallucinate Hadith text and religious rulings. 
   Ad-Diin solves this through strict vector retrieval (RAG) over verified Islamic corpora.

6.3 Technical & Practical Contribution
The primary contribution of Ad-Diin is the seamless fusion of:
Daily Islamic Utilities + Verifiable Philanthropy + Digital Focus + Deen Gamification + RAG-Powered AI
into an enterprise-grade ASP.NET Core (.NET 9) web application.

The standout technical innovation is Diin AI: a decoupled RAG pipeline running 
Qwen3-8B on GPU infrastructure, converting user queries into multilingual semantic 
vectors, retrieving matching Quranic and Hadith records from ChromaDB, and formatting 
strict, citation-backed answers in real time.
